Turn on Tap to Click

Perform a click by tapping the trackpad with one finger instead of pressing it down.

Open this setting

System Settings

Settings path

System Settings › Trackpad › Point & Click › Tap to click

Steps

  1. Open System Settings.
  2. Click Trackpad in the sidebar, then click Point & Click.
  3. Turn on “Tap to click.”
  4. Tap the trackpad in Finder or on a webpage to confirm that a tap performs a click.

Before

When Tap to click is off, you need to press the trackpad to perform a click.

After

When Tap to click is on, tapping the trackpad with one finger performs a click without requiring a physical press.

Undo the change

  • Return to Trackpad > Point & Click, then turn off “Tap to click.”
